Why self-healing locators matter

By Aarohi Sharma · 12 Aug 2026 · 6 min read

Every front-end team keeps a suite of browser tests that depend on selectors. A container rename on a Tuesday turns a green suite red, and the fix is tedious: find the selector, update it, re-run, repeat.

The interesting question is not how to make selectors robust, but what happens when the page changes and nobody is watching. That is where a resolver that scores candidates instead of matching strings earns its keep.

Scoring beats string matching

Compare candidates on weighted features: tag, text, position, neighbours. A single renamed class barely moves the score; a whole redesign correctly fails the threshold.
