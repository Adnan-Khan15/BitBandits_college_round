What we learned shipping weekly

By Kabir Menon · 3 Sep 2026 · 8 min read

Weekly releases force small diffs, and small diffs force honest estimates. After a year of the cadence, the team agrees on one thing: the process matters more than the tooling.

The surprise was social. Reviewing five small changes a week beats reviewing one large change a month, both for attention and for blame.
